# DRMS User Guide — Screen by Screen

A plain-language guide for using the Disaster Response Management System. Every button, menu, and message named here is exactly what you see on screen. Written 2026-09-14 against the current application.

**Who this is for:** anyone who uses DRMS through a web browser — assessors and responders in the field, coordinators in the office, donors, and administrators. No technical knowledge needed.

## 1. Welcome

DRMS helps everyone respond to disasters together:

1. A **disaster incident** is recorded.
2. **Assessors** visit affected communities and fill in assessment forms.
3. **Coordinators** check (verify) those assessments and plan what help is needed.
4. **Donors** promise (commit) items such as food, blankets, or medicine.
5. **Responders** deliver the items and confirm the delivery with location and photos.
6. **Coordinators** verify the delivery — and the community's progress is updated automatically.

Your **dashboard** always shows an **Action Queue** — a to-do list the system builds for you. Whenever something needs your attention, it appears there with a button to go straight to it. You never need to hunt through menus to know what to do next.

## 2. First Steps

### 2.1 Sign in

1. Open DRMS in your browser. You will see the **Login** card: "Enter your credentials to access the disaster management system".
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. If the details are wrong you will see "Invalid email or password". Check your spelling and try again.
5. When it works, you land on your role's dashboard automatically.

There is no "forgot password" button — the sign-in screen says "Contact your administrator for account access". Your administrator can reset your password.

### 2.2 Register your organization (donors only)

If you are a donor organization using DRMS for the first time:

1. Open the **Register** page ("Register Your Organization").
2. **Step 1 of 2 — Organization Information**: fill in **Organization Name** (required), **Organization Type** (Organization, Individual, Government, NGO, or Corporate), and optionally contact email, phone, and details. Click **Next**.
3. **Step 2 of 2 — User Account**: fill in **Contact Name**, **User Email**, **Username**, and a **Password** of at least 8 characters (retype it under **Confirm Password**; the "Passwords match" note confirms you typed it correctly). A "Password strength" meter shows how strong it is.
4. Click **Complete Registration**.
5. You will see "Registration successful! Welcome to the platform." and go straight to your Donor Dashboard, already signed in.

Everyone else (assessors, responders, coordinators, admins) gets an account from their administrator — see [9. Administrator Guide](#9-administrator-guide).

### 2.3 Install the app on your phone or laptop

For field work, install DRMS like a normal app so it works better offline:

1. A floating **Install App** button appears in the app. Tap it.
2. In the "Install DRMS App" window ("Access disaster response tools offline"), click **Install Now** (or **Maybe Later** to postpone).
3. On an iPhone/iPad, the app shows three steps instead: tap the **Share** button in Safari → select **Add to Home Screen** → confirm with **Add**.

The installed app opens from your home screen and can load without internet.

## 3. A Tour of Your Screen

Every page shares the same layout:

**Left side — the menu.** Your menu items depend on your role (each guide below lists them). At the bottom of the menu, everyone has **Profile**, **Help & Support**, and **Logout**.

**Top bar, left to right:**

- The **DRMS logo and name** — click to go home.
- **Sync status** — one of: **Synced** (all your work is safely sent), **Syncing... NN%** (sending now), **N pending** (waiting to send), or **Offline**.
- **Connection pill** — **Online** (green) or **Offline** (red). Hover it to see "Connected to internet. Data will sync automatically." or "No internet connection. Working offline with local data."
- **Notifications bell** — a red badge shows unread count ("99+" when many). Click to open your notifications; use **Mark as read** or **Dismiss** on each.
- **Switch Role** (only if you have more than one role) — see below.
- **Logout** — asks "Are you sure you want to sign out of your account?" with **Sign Out** / **Cancel**.

**Switching roles.** If your account has several roles (for example coordinator and assessor), click the role badge in the top bar and pick another role from the **Switch Role** menu. The app warns about **Unsaved Changes** if you were mid-form and offers **Save & Switch**. Your work in each role is kept separate and restored when you come back.

**Your profile.** Menu → **Profile** shows your role and account status, and an **Edit Profile** form (email, username, full name, phone, organization). To change your password, fill **Current Password** and the new password fields, then click **Update User**.

## 4. Statuses You Will See

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved but not sent yet. You can keep editing. |
| **Pending Review** | Sent and waiting for a coordinator to check it. |
| **Verified** | A coordinator checked and approved it. |
| **Auto-Verified** | Approved automatically (trusted organization). |
| **Rejected** | A coordinator sent it back. Read the reason, fix it, and resubmit. |
| **Planned** | Scheduled but not yet delivered. |
| **Delivered** | The responder confirmed the delivery happened. |
| **Awaiting Verification** | Delivered and waiting for the coordinator's final check. |
| **In Progress** (commitments) | Some of the promised items have been delivered. |
| **Completed** (commitments) | Everything promised was delivered and verified. |
| **Gap / No Gap** | Something is missing in that area (red) or the situation is okay (green). |
| Priority badges **CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW** | How urgent something is. |

## 5. Assessor Guide

Your menu: **Dashboard · Rapid Assessments · Create New Assessment · Preliminary Assessment** — plus Profile, Help & Support, Logout.

### 5.1 Your Dashboard

"Assessor Dashboard — Manage and create rapid assessments for affected communities." Four cards show **Pending Actions**, **Overdue**, **Completed Today**, and **Total Assessments**. Below is your **Action Queue** ("N pending, N critical") — each entry (for example "Reassessment needed" or "Overdue") has a **View** button and a direct action button like **Start Assessment** or **Reassess**, plus a map of where the work is. The queue is empty when you see "All clear".

### 5.2 Do a quick first look (Preliminary Assessment)

Use this right after a disaster for a fast impact snapshot:

1. Menu → **Preliminary Assessment** → **Create New Assessment**.
2. Reporting date and your name fill in automatically.
3. Under **Location Information**, click **Capture GPS** to record where you are (or pick the spot on the map), and choose the LGA/Ward.
4. Under **Impact Assessment**, enter **Lives Lost**, **Injured**, **Displaced**, **Houses Affected**; add schools/clinics affected and any other details.
5. Optionally attach photos and link a related incident, then click **Submit Assessment**. You'll see "Assessment submitted successfully!" and it goes straight to the coordinator's system.

Not done yet? Click **Save Draft** and continue later — the list page offers **Continue Draft** ("You have N saved draft(s)").

### 5.3 Do a full assessment (Rapid Assessment)

1. Menu → **Create New Assessment** (or from the dashboard: pick a type in the **Select assessment type** box — *Health Assessment, Population Assessment, Food Security Assessment, WASH Assessment, Shelter Assessment, Security Assessment* — and click **+ New Assessment**).
2. Choose one of the six type cards (for example **WASH Assessment** — "Water, sanitation, and hygiene conditions").
3. Fill the form top to bottom:
   - **Incident** — which disaster this is about ("Select an active incident").
   - **Assessment Location** — the community you are assessing. If you see "No entities are currently assigned to you", ask your coordinator to assign you.
   - The sector questions — simple Yes/No toggles, counts, and choices. The red **Gap** / green **No Gap** badges update **as you fill it in**, so you can see problems forming live. The sticky bar at the top always shows your current gap count.
   - **GPS Location Capture** — click **Capture GPS**; check the accuracy reading.
   - **Media Attachments** — "Select Photos" or drag and drop (PNG/JPG/WebP, up to 10MB each, max 5).
4. Your progress is saved automatically every 30 seconds ("Draft saved successfully"), and you can also click **Save Draft**.
5. Click the submit button at the bottom (for example **Submit WASH Assessment**).
6. You return to your list. The new assessment shows as **Pending Review** (or **Auto-Verified** for trusted organizations).

**If a previous assessment of the same type exists**, the form opens with "Reassessment — Previous Data Loaded" and the old answers filled in for you to update.

### 5.4 After submitting

- View any assessment's full details with **View Details** (gaps, severity, recommendations, sync status).
- You cannot edit a submitted assessment. If the coordinator rejects it, your list shows **Rejected** with an **Edit** button and a **Reason** button that opens "The reason provided by the coordinator for rejecting this assessment". Fix the problem, then submit again (the incident and community are locked on a resubmission).
- Use the **Filters** on your list (entity, type, status, priority, date) to find things fast.

## 6. Responder Guide

Your menu: **Dashboard · Response Planning (Create Response, Response Deliveries, Response Plans)** — plus Profile, Help & Support, Logout.

### 6.1 Your Dashboard

"Response Dashboard — Plan and manage response operations for affected communities." Cards show **Pending Actions**, **Awaiting Delivery**, **Partially Covered**, and **Active Plans**; below, your **Action Queue** with buttons like **Create Plan** or **Confirm Delivery** that take you straight to the job. **View Response Deliveries** (or **Deliveries** on a phone) jumps to your delivery list.

### 6.2 Plan a response

1. Menu → **Create Response** (or **Create Plan** on a queue item).
2. On the "Create Response Plan" form pick the **Entity**, then the **Assessment** it's based on. **Response Type** and **Priority** fill in automatically from the assessment.
3. Under **Resources**, add each item: **Item Name** (e.g. "Clean water bottles"), **Quantity**, **Unit**. Click **+ Add Item** for more.
4. Click **Create Plan** — or **Create Delivery Directly** if you're delivering right now.
5. Instead of typing items by hand, the **Import from Commitment** tab lets you build the plan from what a donor has already promised.

If a plan gets rejected, the **Edit** button opens "Edit Rejected Response" showing the **Rejection Reason** — fix and click **Update Response**.

### 6.3 Confirm a delivery

When you have physically delivered the items:

1. Menu → **Response Deliveries**, find the **Planned** item, click **Confirm Delivery**.
2. On the "Confirm Delivery" page ("Document the actual delivery of planned aid items with location verification and photos"):
   - **Delivery Location** — your GPS position is captured automatically; you can press **Capture Location** again for a fresh fix. Check the Latitude/Longitude/Accuracy shown.
   - **Delivered Items** — click **Edit Items** if you delivered different amounts than planned.
   - **Delivery Notes** — anything the coordinator should know.
   - **Delivery Photos** — click **Take/Select Photos** (max 5, up to 10MB each).
3. Click **Preview** to double-check, then click **Confirm Delivery**.
4. You'll see "**Delivery Confirmed!** — Your delivery has been successfully documented and submitted for verification", and the item moves to **Awaiting Verification** for the coordinator.

This works offline too — see [10. Working Offline](#10-working-offline).

## 7. Donor Guide

Your menu: **Dashboard · My Commitments (Manage Commitments, Create New Commitment, Commitment Status) · Assigned Entities (Entity Locations, Entity Performance) · Performance & Analytics (Performance Dashboard, Achievements, Leaderboard, Analytics)** — plus Profile, Help & Support, Logout.

### 7.1 Your Dashboard

"Donor Dashboard." Cards: **Pending Actions**, **My Active Commitments**, **Partially Fulfilled**, **Fulfilled Today**. Your **Action Queue** highlights communities that need help, each with a **Make Commitment** button that opens the commitment form with the details pre-filled.

### 7.2 Promise help (Create a Commitment)

1. Click **Make Commitment** on a queue item, or menu → **Create New Commitment**.
2. On "Create New Commitment — Specify the items you're committing to donate for this incident": pick the **Target Entity** and **Incident**.
3. Under **Commitment Items**, add rows: **Item Name** (e.g. "Rice, Blankets"), **Quantity**, **Unit**, and optionally **Est. Value**. Click **Add Item** for more rows.
4. Tip: the **From Response Plan** tab lists exactly what a community still needs — items import with the missing quantities pre-filled ("you can adjust them or commit more").
5. Click **Create Commitment**. You'll see "Commitment created successfully!"

### 7.3 Track your commitments

- **Manage Commitments** ("My Commitments") lists everything with **Delivery Progress** bars ("delivered / total") and statuses **Planned → In Progress → Completed**. Filter by status, incident, or entity.
- **Commitment Status** ("All Commitment Status") shows each response your commitment feeds: **Planned**, **Delivered**, **Verified**, marked **Your Donation**, with "From Commitment #…".

### 7.4 See how you're doing

- **Performance Dashboard**: **Delivery Rate**, **Total Commitments**, **Total Value**, **Current Rank**, plus trend charts (Line/Bar/Radar) you can switch between.
- **Achievements**: badges in four families — **Reliable Delivery** (70/85/95% verified delivery rate for Bronze/Silver/Gold), **High Volume** (10+/25+/50+ completed commitments), **Quick Response** (responding on average under 24/12/6 hours), **Consistency** (active 3+ months in a row).
- **Leaderboard**: all donors ranked. Click **Ranked by** to open "How Rankings Are Calculated" — the score blends **Delivery**, **Response Speed**, **Commitment Value**, and **Consistency**; ties share a rank and it updates every 15 minutes.
- **Assigned Entities**: communities linked to you — see their assessments, gaps, and **Critical Gaps Identified** per entity.

### 7.5 Reports

Menu → **Reports** (Donor Reports): choose a **Report Format** (PDF / CSV / EXCEL), a **Date Range**, tick **Include Charts / Trends Analysis / Gap Analysis** if you want them, select entities, and click **Generate Report**. The file downloads to your device. (See [12. Good to Know](#12-good-to-know) about formats.)

## 8. Coordinator Guide

Your menu: **Crisis Dashboard · Situation Awareness · Signal Analytics · Operations Mgmt (Verification Queue, Verification Metrics, Entity Assignment, Entity Management, Resource Allocation, Incident Management) · Donor Relations (Donor Management, Donor Metrics) · Configuration (Auto-Approval Management, Gap Field Management, Severity Thresholds, Scoring Configuration) · Reports (Report Management, Report Builder) · Mapping & Visualization (Entity-Incident Map)**.

### 8.1 Your Dashboard

"Coordinator Dashboard." Cards: **Assessments to Verify**, **Deliveries to Verify**, **All Pending Actions**, **Verification Overdue**. Your Action Queue entries (like "Assessment to verify", "Delivery to verify", "Needs responder assigned") carry buttons such as **Review Assessment**, **Review Delivery**, **Assign Responder**, **Assign Donor**.

### 8.2 Verify assessments

1. Menu → **Verification Queue** ("Verification Queue" page, tab **Assessments**).
2. Find a **Pending** item (use the search and **Status** filter). Expand it to see **Entity Details**, **Assessor Details**, and the **Timeline**. An "Auto-Approve Enabled" badge marks trusted organizations.
3. To accept: **Approve Assessment** → optionally add **Verification Notes** → click **Approve Assessment**.
4. To send back: **Reject Assessment** → choose a **Rejection Reason** (Incomplete Data, Inaccurate Information, Missing Documentation, Location Mismatch, Duplicate Assessment, Quality Issues, Other) → write the required **Feedback** ("Explain why this assessment is being rejected and what needs to be corrected") → click **Reject Assessment**. The assessor sees exactly this reason and can resubmit.

The **Responses** tab works the same for deliveries: **Verify Response** / **Reject Response** (reason required). The **Analytics** tab shows your **Verification Rate**, **Avg Processing Time**, and **Rejection Rate**.

### 8.3 Manage incidents

1. Menu → **Incident Management**.
2. **New Incident**: fill **Incident Type**, **Sub-Type**, **Status**, **Location** (with **Get GPS location**), **Description** — you can attach an existing preliminary assessment here — then **Create Incident**.
3. On each row: change status (**Active / Contained / Resolved**), **View Details**, **Link Assessment** (opens "Manage Preliminary Assessments" — select from "Available Assessments" and click **Link Selected**), or **Delete**.
4. Severity and priority are calculated for you from verified assessments — you never type them.

### 8.4 Turn donor promises into deliveries

Use **Import from Donor Commitment** ("Import from Donor Commitment"): pick the **Target Entity** and **Incident**, choose from **Orphaned Commitments** (promises not yet attached to a plan), adjust item quantities with the +/− steppers, **Preview Import**, then **Create Plan**. The system checks availability so nothing gets double-promised. Assign responders/donors to communities under **Entity Assignment** / **Entity Management**.

### 8.5 See the big picture

- **Situation Awareness** ("Situation Dashboard"): incident overview, communities and their gaps, **Operational Metrics**, **Top Performing Donors**, and a map. Toggle **Coordinator / Executive** mode for a simpler or more detailed view; **Export** as "CSV (Excel)", "Excel (.xlsx)", or "PDF Report".
- **Signal Analytics** with CSV export; **Entity-Incident Map** for who-is-affected-where.

### 8.6 Tune how scoring works (Configuration)

- **Scoring Configuration**: the four weights (**Delivery / Speed / Value / Consistency**) must total exactly 100% — the "Weight Total" line and live preview help. **Save Changes**, **Discard**, or **Defaults**.
- **Severity Thresholds**: per severity level (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL), set minimums for **Lives Lost**, **Injured**, **Displaced**.
- **Gap Field Management**: per sector tab (Health / Food / WASH / Shelter / Security), set each question's gap severity; **Save All**, then **Recalculate All** to refresh every existing assessment ("Recalculation complete — Assessments updated and incident severities recalculated.").
- **Auto-Approval Management**: mark trusted organizations so their assessments skip your queue.

## 9. Administrator Guide

Your menu: **Dashboard · User Management (All Users, Role Management) · Donor Management (All Donors, Register New Donor) · Reports (Report Management, Report Builder) · System Administration (System Settings, Audit Logs, Database Management, System Health, Notification Settings, Map Settings, Brand Settings, Currency Settings)**.

### 9.1 Dashboard

"Admin Dashboard" with **Total Users**, **Active Incidents**, **Total Entities**, **Donors**, **Pending Verifications**, and more; quick links jump straight to **Manage Users**, **Role Management**, **System Settings**, **Database Management**, **System Health**.

### 9.2 Create and manage user accounts

1. Menu → **All Users** ("User Management") → **Create User**.
2. In "Create New User": fill Email, Username, Password, Full Name, optional phone/organization, and tick the **Roles**. (DONOR accounts are created through donor self-registration or **Register New Donor** — the form notes this.)
3. Click **Create User**.
4. **Edit User** on any row changes details, toggles **Active User** off to deactivate, adjusts roles, or resets the password.

### 9.3 Roles and permissions

Menu → **Role Management** ("Role Management"): **Create Role**, or **Edit Role** on a row, then tick permissions (grouped by category) in the matrix. The table shows each role's permission count and user count.

### 9.4 System settings

- **Brand Settings**: the app **App Name**, description, header icon, PWA icon, and the **Login Page Logo** everyone sees at sign-in.
- **Currency Settings**: the display currency and symbol (e.g. "NGN (₦) — Nigerian Naira").
- **Map Settings**: default Latitude, Longitude, and **Default Zoom Level**.
- **Notification Settings**: channels (Push / In-App / Email), priority routing, quiet hours, and per-role dashboard alerts.
- **System Settings**: general settings.
- **System Health**: live status cards — Database Status (Healthy/Degraded), API Response Time, Storage Usage, Last Backup, and Service Status for Web Server, Database, Authentication, File Storage.
- **Audit Logs** ("Audit Trail & Monitoring"): who did what and when; filter or **Export CSV**.
- **Database Management**: tabs **Overview / Backup & Restore / Tables / Query / Maintenance**. Under **Backup & Restore**: **Create Backup**, then per backup **Download**, **Restore**, or **Delete** (with confirmation).

## 10. Working Offline

Field work often happens without signal. DRMS is built for that:

**Before you go offline**, open the assessment forms once while connected. If more data is needed, a friendly screen appears: "Offline Data Required" with a checklist (Entities and locations, Active incidents, Assessment templates) — click **Download Data** and wait for the progress bar to finish.

**While offline:**

- The connection pill shows **Offline**. Everything you type is stored safely on your device (it is encrypted — nobody can read it off a lost phone).
- You can still fill and submit **assessments**, **response plans**, and **delivery confirmations**. Forms note: "You are currently offline. Your response plan will be saved locally and synced when you reconnect."
- Delivery confirmations tell you: "Delivery confirmation stored for offline sync. It will be automatically synced when you're back online."

**When you're back online:** the pill turns **Online** and your queued work sends itself automatically — the sync indicator runs through **Syncing...** to **Synced**, and a small notification may announce "Sync Complete — Synced N items successfully".

**Important limits offline:** you cannot see *new* information from the office while offline (your device does not pull fresh data), dashboards and reports need a connection, and if two people changed the same thing, the server's copy wins.

## 11. Help & Troubleshooting

Menu → **Help & Support** ("Help & Support") has quick links (Dashboard, Your Profile, System Status), email support at **support@dms-system.org**, and FAQs about creating assessments, donor commitments, verification, reports, users, and roles.

Common situations:

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Invalid email or password" | Retype carefully; passwords are case-sensitive. Still stuck — ask your administrator to reset it. |
| Sent to the login page with an error | Your session expired; just sign in again — you'll return where you were. |
| A form won't submit | Look for red messages under fields; the submit button also blocks while required fields are empty. |
| Your assessment was **Rejected** | Open the **Reason** button, read the coordinator's feedback, click **Edit**, fix it, submit again. |
| Stuck on "Offline Data Required" | Get to a connection, click **Download Data**, wait for 100%. |
| Photos say "Maximum photo limit reached" | Up to 5 photos per form, 10MB each. Remove one to add another. |
| Queue item won't go away after you acted | Click the page's **Refresh** button; the queue updates when the system recalculates. |
| Something looks wrong on screen | Reload the page (F5 / pull down). Your saved work is not lost. |

## 12. Good to Know

Honest notes about things that are not finished yet, so you're not surprised:

1. **Photos don't reach the office yet.** Assessment and delivery photos are stored on your device, and the forms promise they'll sync — but photo transfer to the server is not switched on yet. Always keep your own copies of important photo evidence.
2. **Donor report formats:** only **CSV** downloads a proper spreadsheet right now. Choosing **PDF** or **EXCEL** on Donor Reports gives you a data file instead. (Coordinator/Admin **Report Builder** does produce real PDF/Excel.)
3. **One-way offline sync.** Offline devices send their work in, but can't receive fresh data back yet.
4. **Resource Allocation page** (Coordinator → Operations Mgmt) is a display only; its buttons don't do anything yet.
5. **No lockout:** wrong password attempts are unlimited — make yours long, and tell your administrator immediately if you think someone else knows it.
6. **Deactivated accounts** may show a technical error instead of a polite message; that's known and being fixed.
